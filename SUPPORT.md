# Support

Start with the [manual](docs/index.md), the
[troubleshooting guide](docs/troubleshooting.md), and the
[compatibility notes](docs/compatibility.md).

For a reproducible bug, open a GitHub issue and include:

- the nbshell revision or release;
- the active compositor and its version;
- the Quickshell version;
- concise reproduction steps; and
- sanitized output from `nbshell system-report` when it is relevant.

Remove names, paths, device identifiers, addresses, account data, tokens, and
private message content before posting. General support is also available at
`support@nbsystems.dev`.

Do not report vulnerabilities through support or a public issue. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Privacy questions belong at
`privacy@nbsystems.dev` or through [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
