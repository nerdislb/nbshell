# Midjourney wallpaper provenance

These 24 original wallpapers were directed for nbshell and generated by the
project owner with a paid Midjourney Pro account on 2026-09-02. Prompts required
original landscapes or city scenes with no text, logos, watermarks, recognizable
third-party branding, characters, or literal user-interface reproductions.

The selected 2944×1648 sRGB PNG generations were center-cropped to 16:9, resized
to 1920×1080, stripped of metadata, and encoded as quality-88 WebP files for
nbshell. The project owner contributes the resulting assets for distribution
under this repository's MIT license. Original prompts and source generations are
retained in the creator's `nbshell-wallpapers` production workspace.
